Sunday 22nd November 2026

Windy across upland Britain, gales some higher areas, particularly Snowdonia. Extensively dry and cold and much terrain will be at least partially frozen. However, cloud progressively thicker southward across Wales, with an almost stationary area of rain South Wales.

Colder than normal most mountain areas throughout next week. Precipitation and temporarily warmer conditions will occasionally penetrate across most southern and western mountains, but particularly later in the week, freezing level across Britain may drop to a few hundred metres - with much higher terrain persistently frozen. Most considerable snowfall is likely over the SE Highlands and perhaps Scottish Border hills.
